Dear Youth Pastor or Leader,

I'd like to invite you and your students to the Authentic Youth Conference, hosted by Vision Baptist Church on Saturday, March 20, 2027.

Our students are growing up in a world full of filters and facades. Much of what they see every day is edited to look better than it really is, and it's easy for their faith to start working the same way. Authentic is a one-day conference built to challenge them to live out a real, Christ-centered faith.

We've planned the day around clear, engaging Bible teaching, meaningful worship, and intentional fellowship, all aimed at encouraging students to follow Jesus for real. Pastor Rick Finley will bring the keynote messages, and Dr. Mike Lester will join us as our guest speaker. Both men love teenagers and preach God's Word with clarity and conviction.

We also want your students to have a great time. The day includes great food, fun games, exciting giveaways, and a full coffee bar. The conference is open to all middle and high school students, so bring your whole group.
